Platform

A world model for living tissue.

Ymir predicts how a patient's tissue reacts before a doctor treats. It is built from five modules, and it couples cells and molecular signals with tissue physics such as cell mechanics and local pressure.

01Knowledge

Atlas

A library of 9,008 curated mathematical forms (5,625 distinct equations) for how cells grow, signal, move and remodel tissue — each checked against its source paper.

02Assembly

Builder

AI agents work with a scientist to assemble a disease-specific model — a “mechanism chip.”

03Forecast

Predict

Simulates thousands of cells and molecules to forecast tumor growth, healing, scarring and immune response.

04Intervene

Act

Applies treatments to the twin from 11 treatment classes, spanning drug therapy, local treatment such as radiation, and surgery. Guided by ASCO clinical practice recommendations.

05Interpret

Explain

Links predictions to named biological mechanisms and matches each virtual cell to measured gene-expression profiles from reference atlases.
